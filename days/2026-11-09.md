# Why I started
Bitcoin is a scum, M-Pesa is enough for my clients,
nobody ever asks to pay me with bitcoin, is it legal?

These were the obstacles when delivering a bitcoin wallet to a merchant in Kisumu.

I went back to my developer community and created a QR code to autoconvert
the bitcoin sent to these local businesses so that they receive it in a way
they already trust, M-Pesa, but it was also not so easy, but 2 merchants said yes.

Now getting these merchants customers willing to pay in bitcoin is my goal.
My aim is to show my friends that anyone can be paid doing what they are already doin.

Open source is our easiest path to geting paid in bitcoin as Golang developers,
I started this to help create developers who will build the infrustructure Kisumu needs for the future of money.
